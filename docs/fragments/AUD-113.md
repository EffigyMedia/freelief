### AUD-113 - Background music and rain keep scheduling notes into the paused audio clock while urgent help is open or the app is hidden, and all of them start together when the person comes back

- Severity: High
- Area: C. Edge cases (interruption mid-operation)
- Evidence: Code, quoted in round UNT-104. app.js help click: `audio.applySound(false); dialog.showModal();`. The visibilitychange handler calls `audio.applySound(false)` when the page is hidden. audio.js applySound(false) sets `held = !on` and then runs `context.suspend()` after the fade. The background loops never check `held`. pads() reschedules itself with `timer = setTimeout(playChord, settings.chordSeconds * 1000)`, and each chord starts oscillators at `const start = ctx.currentTime`. rain() does the same with `timer = setTimeout(drop, settings.dropEveryMs * ...)` and `tick.start(now, ...)`. While the context is suspended, currentTime does not move, so every chord gets the same start time. Scratch probe, round UNT-104: a harness.open_app page with an init script that records OscillatorNode.start. Steps: click once, open #calm (music), open Need urgent help?, wait 32 s. Result: `triangle oscillators started while help open: {'n': 30, 'states': ['suspended'], 'starts': ['1.733'], ...}`. That is three chords of 10 voices, all at the one frozen time 1.733. After 'Back', the context was 'running' and all of them played at once. config.json: pads chordSeconds 10, volume 0.04; rain dropEveryMs 300. No test covers sound after help has been open for some time. (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: This hurts a person who opens urgent help while the Visualizer's music or nature sound plays. The sound plays on every screen since RLG-045. The person reads the lines or leaves the app to call one (AUD-074 names this case). When they come back, every chord that was scheduled in the meantime starts in phase. That is about 6 chords a minute with help open, so 5 minutes gives about 30 chords, or about 300 oscillators. The amplitudes add, and the result is a loud, clipped chord of about 18 s. With rain or Both, hundreds of noise ticks are also queued at one instant. This happens on the never-break help path, just after a crisis call, in an app that must calm. The tests pass, so only an owner phone check could catch it. On Android, a hidden page may be frozen after some minutes, which limits the hidden case. The help-open case has no such limit.
- Direction: Stop the background loops while sound is held, and start them again when it is released. Do not only suspend the clock. For example, have applySound(false) call background stop, and have applySound(true) call background.restart(). Alternatively, make playChord and drop return early when `held` is true. Add a test that holds help open over two chord periods, closes it, and asserts that no more than one chord's voices start within one chord period. Add the owner's phone check (help open for a few minutes, then close) to RLG-033.
