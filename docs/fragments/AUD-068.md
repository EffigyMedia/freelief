### AUD-068 - The Delivery Plan ends at slice 5, so 31 versions of new features belong to no slice, and the slice-end audit pace and the minor bump never fire

- Severity: Medium
- Area: G. Process amendments
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. Design Stage 10 defines slices 1 to 5 only. Since v0.5.0 the line has shipped v0.5.1 to v0.5.36 as patches, including three new requirements (REQ-032 ripple pond, REQ-033 mandala, REQ-034 vibration), the Visualizer, the region setting and the haptics module. AGENTS.md: "A finished slice is a **minor** bump." and "**Audit pace:** run an audit round at the end of each slice". The round now running is titled "Design audit round after slice-6 owner work (v0.5.36)", but no slice 6 is defined anywhere. No round ran between UNT-010 and UNT-051. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The project's own audit pace depends on slice ends, and work outside slices never reaches one. The roughly 31 features since the last round were built without the milestone check that the pace promises. The version number no longer shows where a body of work ended.
- Direction: The owner rules on one of two options. (a) Add the post-slice-5 work to the Delivery Plan as named slices with done-when lines, and bump the minor version at each slice end. (b) Amend the audit pace in AGENTS.md and the Instruction Changelog to a trigger that fires without slices, for example every N features or every new requirement. In both cases, record which slice or milestone this round closes.
