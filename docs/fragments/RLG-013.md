### RLG-013 — Ripple pond activity: touch still water and soft ripples spread
- **RULED 2026-10-07T23:32:09-04:00** — Built in UNT-037 as v0.5.23. activities/ripple.js: the pond is one button; pointerdown makes a ripple of three rings at the touch, a drag adds one every 48 px (ripple.trailSpacing), at most 9 show (maxRipples), and a click with no pointer down in the last 600 ms (a key or a screen reader) makes one at a random place inside a 48 px margin. audio.drop() is a rising sine 'plip' (sounds.drop). Reduced motion: rings fade at half size; .ripple-ring is exempt from the global zero-duration rule, as .calm-shape is. Routes, menu, service worker, a11y and preview lists include it. test_ripple.py: tap position and clear-away, drag trail and cap, keyboard and name, one ripple per tap, reduced motion, drop sound. A debug run found that the click guard started at time 0 and swallowed a key press in the first 600 ms; it now starts at -Infinity. 95 tests; 178.1 KB; bench 157 ms and 65 ms. How the ripples and the drop feel is for the owner to check on a phone.
