### RLG-041 — Ripple pond: ripples interact mathematically (interference: brighter where rings meet, fainter where they cancel), DOM or SVG only
- **RULED 2026-10-09T08:04:19-04:00** — 2026-10-09 (UNT-095): built in v0.7.7. activities/ripple.js draws the water as a grid of SVG dots (10 px apart, about 1200 on a phone); each dot's opacity is a rest level plus waveHeight(), the sum of one travelling, fading wave packet per ripple. Only visible changes are written, and no frame runs on still water (tested). A test checks the exact sum, crests meeting at about twice one crest, and a crest-trough meeting cancelling to under 35%; it fails if the sum is replaced by a maximum. Reduced motion keeps the fading rings. Forced colors draw the dots in CanvasText. 163 tests pass.
