### REQ-007 — Freelief plays subtle sounds: a soft tone that lasts each breathing phase, and short cues for the activities (a bubble pop, a singing-glass tone while tracing, a soft tick and a rising phrase in Unblock, a water drop in the pond, a chime for each mandala color, and the background music, rain and waves). Every pitched sound that plays with the music is in its key, C major. Sounds are on by default; the speaker button in the header fades them all out over a fraction of a second and stops them. Nothing plays before the person's first tap or key press.

**History.**
- 2026-10-07 (agreed in the design interview): "Freelief can play soft tones that mark breath in and
  breath out. The tones are off by default and the person can turn them on."
- 2026-10-07 (owner, UNT-016): changed. The owner asked for more sounds, subtle, on by default, with
  a switch to turn them off; for breathing tones that last the duration of each phase; and for the
  shape trace to sound like rubbing a crystal glass. Browsers allow sound only after a gesture, so
  the first tap or key press unlocks it.
- 2026-10-08 (AUD-065): "a chime on Next" removed, because the grounding screen that had it was
  removed on 2026-10-07 (UNT-032). "One Sounds switch in Settings" became "the speaker button in the
  header", which the owner chose on 2026-10-08 (UNT-053). The behavior does not change.
- 2026-10-09 (AUD-090; UNT-101): was "...(... a click and a rising phrase in the colour sort). ... turns them all off at once." The owner asked for a fade in place of the hard cut (RLG-034, UNT-078, 2026-10-08), and the text now says so. The cue list names Unblock (it replaced the colour sort), the pond, the mandala and the background sound, and the C-major rule (RLG-046).
