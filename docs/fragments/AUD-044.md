### AUD-044 - The urgent-help dialog can be closed by touch only from its bottom, after all the content

- Severity: Low
- Area: F. User experience
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. app.js: `body.push(details, close);`, so Close is the last element. styles.css: `.close { margin-top: 1rem; width: 100%; }`, which is not sticky. On a 360x640 screen the dialog content runs past the fold before Close is reached. A native dialog does not close on a backdrop tap. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: A touch user who opened help by mistake, or is done with it, must scroll the whole list to return to breathing. Escape works only with a keyboard.
- Direction: Add a close control at the top of the dialog, or make Close sticky. The owner checks the result on a phone.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-010]].
- **RULED 2026-10-07T19:07:38-04:00** — Attempt 1, UNT-015 (v0.5.3), prompted by the owner on the phone ('I cant exit the Urgent help window'): the bottom Close button is replaced by a Back button in a sticky header at the top of the dialog, and opening the dialog pushes a history entry so the phone's back gesture closes it. test_feedback_phone.py checks that Back sits at the top and stays visible after scrolling, that the back gesture closes the dialog and stays in the app, and that no extra history step is left. A later round confirms.
- **Re-checked 2026-10-08T01:22:24-04:00 by round [[UNT-051]]:** confirmed fixed.
  - the whole-design lens: done - app.js puts `back` (class "button help-back") in `element("div", { class: "help-header" }, [ ...h2, back ])` before the body. styles.css: `.help-header { position: sticky; top: 0; ...}`. test_feedback_phone.py has test_the_way_out_of_urgent_help_is_at_the_top_and_stays_visible and test_the_phone_back_gesture_closes_urgent_help_and_stays_in_the_app.
