### AUD-019 - The emergency number in the urgent-help dialog is plain text, not a tap-to-call link

- Severity: Medium
- Area: F. User experience
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. app.js buildHelpDialog: `element("p", { class: "emergency", text: own ? t("help.emergency", { number: own.emergency }) ... })`. It is a paragraph with no `tel:` link. The crisis lines below it get `href: \`tel:${dial(line.call)}\``. The 360x640 screenshot shows "If you are in immediate danger, call 911." as bold text with no button. Design Stage 3 principle 1: "Help first. Nothing stands between the person and help". (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: A person in immediate danger, with shaky hands, must leave the app and dial the most urgent number by hand, while a lower-priority line one row down has a one-tap button.
- Direction: Make the emergency number a tel: button in the same style as the line buttons. For a region with two numbers, such as IE "112 or 999", store them as a list and give each its own button. Add a test.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-010]].

Test: tools/tests/test_safety.py test_the_emergency_number_is_the_first_thing_to_tap.
