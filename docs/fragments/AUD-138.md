### AUD-138 - The design, AGENTS.md and the generated SRS still give the Visualizer a sound choice of its own and a stored sound, which RLG-049 removed

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Design alignment
- Evidence: Quoted in this round. Design section 8, module table: "`activities/calm.js` (**the Visualizer**) | The choice of background sound, saved and played by the shell, soft shapes that fade in and out". F7: "sound on or off is the speaker in the header; the Visualizer's sound choice is kept too." Section 11, risk table: "a reset never starts it; only a choice on the Kaleidoscope starts it (REQ-036)." srs-product-overview, which is copied into docs/SRS.md: "the Visualizer (music, rain or waves with soft shapes)". AGENTS.md Architecture: "**The Visualizer** (`activities/calm.js`, route `#calm`) — music, nature (rain or waves) or both, with soft shapes". The 2026-10-09 UNT-105 Decision Log entry says "Now only a choice on the Visualizer starts a sound" and has no Superseded note. Against these: design section 4 says the Visualizer "has no sound choice of its own; the sound bar plays sound on every screen". F5 says "Nothing about it is stored, so each visit starts silent" (omission lens). The UNT-108 entry says "`calmMode` and `natureSound` are no longer stored, and each visit starts silent". REQ-036 says "Background sound is chosen on every screen in the header's sound bar ... nothing about it is stored". activities/calm.js says "this screen has no sound choice of its own". app.js starts a sound from any screen: "background.toggle(sound)". The omission lens found the same calm.js row, F7 clause and risk row as part of a wider sweep of removed screens. (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: A later session that builds from the module table, F7 or AGENTS.md can put a sound choice back on the Kaleidoscope, or store what plays. Either one reverses an owner decision. A reader of F7 or the calm.js row believes a sound choice is stored, which the privacy text denies. The risk table states a sound mitigation that is not how sound now starts, so a reader cannot trust it. The SRS, a public document, describes sound that the screen does not have.
- Direction: Rewrite the calm.js row of the section 8 table, the F7 clause, the background-sound risk row ("only a tap on a sound-bar button starts it"), the srs-product-overview sentence and the AGENTS.md Architecture line so that they match REQ-036 and say that nothing about sound is stored. Add a Superseded note to the UNT-105 entry that points to the UNT-108 entry. Regenerate SRS.md and PRD.md. Delete each replaced sentence in the same edit.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design and omission lenses of round [[UNT-124]] - convergence.
