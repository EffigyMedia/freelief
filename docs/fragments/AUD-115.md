### AUD-115 - The never-break 'Need urgent help?' response is at its 100 ms target, and no valid measurement exists for v0.7.13

- Severity: Medium
- Area: D. Performance
- Evidence: Round UNT-104 ran `python tools/freelief.py bench`: '[FLAG] Input to visible response (REQ-027): 93.9 ms (target 100 ms; baseline 74 ms, +27%)' and 'Response spread 3.6 ms; worst 99.4 ms'. typeperf read about 16% CPU load just before. The health check's three runs gave medians of 95, 98.9 and 101.8 ms, and the last is '[FAIL]'. docs/performance/log.md, v0.7.11: 'on this busy run it had 3.5 ms of headroom'. Since AUD-087 the probe ends after the paint, so the 74 ms baseline is not comparable ('The 74 ms baseline used the old end point' in baseline.md). Every run is marked not valid by the defective load probe (see the AUD-079 recheck). Confidence: four medians, one over the target, all under 4x CPU throttling on a desktop stand-in. If a valid run's median is over 100 ms, this becomes High. (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: This hurts a person who taps 'Need urgent help?' on a mid-range phone. The response may already be over the REQ-027 target. Any growth in the help dialog crosses it. The release step 'run bench on a quiet machine' cannot produce the evidence today, so a release would ship with no known headroom on its most important control.
- Direction: First fix the bench load probe (AUD-079). Then re-baseline on this machine and record the result in docs/performance/log.md and baseline.md. Profile the help open under 4x throttling: showRegion() rebuilds the region block and the emergency buttons on every open. Cut the work until the median has clear headroom, for example 20 ms. Record the result in the log.
