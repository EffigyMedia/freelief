### RLG-026 — Sound switch moves from Settings to a header speaker button, crossed out when off
- **RULED 2026-10-08T01:39:59-04:00** — Built in UNT-053 as v0.5.38. app.js adds a header toggle button (class sound-toggle, aria-label Sound, aria-pressed) between the help button and the gear, with an inline SVG speaker whose waves or cross show by aria-pressed. A setting change calls audio.applySound (off suspends the AudioContext, on unlocks and resumes) and the current screen's soundChanged hook; the Visualizer restarts or stops its music and its note. The Settings switch and its strings are removed. Below 430 px the header tightens (help 16 px, brand 16 px, round buttons 44 px) so the help label stays on one line from 360 px. Tests: off suspends the context at once and on restarts the Visualizer music (proven: fails when the suspend is removed), keyboard order and toggling, one-line help at 360 px, no Settings switch; four tests now use the header button. 113 tests.
