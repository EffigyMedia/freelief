### AUD-155 - Zen Garden gives two items the same name after a removal

- Severity: Low
- Area: F. User experience
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-124. activities/garden.js add(): 'const count = current.items.filter((item) => item.kind === kind).length;' and 'number: count + 1'. The garden starts with three stones (Stone 1, 2, 3). Remove Stone 1, then press Add a stone: count is 2, so the new stone is 'Stone 3', the same as the stone already there. The status says 'Added: Stone 3, ...' (garden.added) and Tab reaches two buttons named 'Stone 3, ...', told apart only by their percent position. test_garden.py removes items but never adds one after a removal. (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: A screen-reader user, who knows the items only by their names, hears two 'Stone 3's and cannot tell which one they chose or moved without comparing positions. It is small, but the design says every item is named so the activity works without sight.
- Direction: Number a new item one past the highest number of its kind ever used in this visit (a counter per kind), or reuse the lowest free number. Add a test: remove a stone, add a stone, and assert that every item's name is unique.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-124]].
