### RLG-021 — Ripple pond: a more muted water drop
- **RULED 2026-10-08T00:14:02-04:00** — Built in UNT-043 as v0.5.29. The drop is lower (360 to 760 Hz, was 520 to 1250), quieter (0.014, was 0.022), a little longer (0.4 s, rise 0.11 s) and passes a 700 Hz low-pass filter (sounds.drop.lowpassHz, new). Test: the last filter made on a ripple is a low-pass at the configured cutoff, below the drop's top pitch. 106 tests. How muted it sounds is for the owner to judge on a phone.
