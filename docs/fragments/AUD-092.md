### AUD-092 - The design's definitions of Settings omit the 'When Freelief opens' setting that settings.js stores

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082 at 82a1c6d. settings.js validators include `openOn: (v) => config.settings.openOnChoices.includes(v)`. Design section 4 defines **Settings** as "The only stored data, and only the values the person changed: the breathing rhythm, sound on or off, vibration on or off, the light or dark theme, the Visualizer's sound (music, rain or both), and the region for urgent help". The srs-definitions region and Stage 8 'Data and state' give the same six items. F7 lists "Rhythm preset, theme override, vibration on or off, and the region for urgent help" and omits both 'When Freelief opens' and Version / Update now, which screens/settings.js shows. About's privacy2 already lists "where it opens". (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: The design's 'only stored data' definition, which REQ-015 relies on and which SRS.md regenerates, lists six keys while the store holds seven. A later privacy review that works from the design misses one. AUD-067 fixed this class at v0.5.44, and UNT-061 (v0.5.46) added openOn without updating these clauses.
- Direction: Add openOn to section 4, srs-definitions, Stage 8 'Data and state' and F7. Add Version and Update now to F7. Regenerate the SRS. Consider a repo test that compares the settings.js validator keys with the list in the design and in about.privacy2.
- Effort: S
