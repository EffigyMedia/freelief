### AUD-082 - A breathing tone or any other cue resumes the suspended audio while urgent help is open or the app is hidden, so audio keeps running silently in the background

- Severity: Low
- Area: D. Performance & resources
- Evidence: audio.js (quoted in round UNT-082): applySound(false) fades the output to 0 and then `context.suspend()`. The comment says 'Off fades every sound out and then pauses the audio'. But ensureContext() does `if (context.state === "suspended") context.resume();`, and cue(), play(), pop(), drop(), pads() and rain() all call it while `canPlay()` is true, because the sounds setting stays on. Scratch probe on #breathe, after a tap, with createGain wrapped to read the master gain. With help open, 7 s later: ['running', '0.000']. After a hidden visibilitychange: at 1 s ['suspended', '0.000'], then from 2 s to 8 s ['running', '0.000']. test_sound_waits_while_urgent_help_is_open_and_while_the_app_is_hidden runs on #calm and checks only within 2 s, so it does not see the resume. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: Nothing is audible, because the master gain stays at 0. But the audio thread runs, and breathing keeps creating oscillators while the person is on a call to a crisis line with Freelief in the background. That uses CPU and battery, and it is not the paused state that the code and AUD-074's fix describe.
- Direction: Keep a 'held silent' flag in audio.js that applySound(false) sets and applySound(true) clears. Make ensureContext() and the sound makers do nothing, with no resume, while it is set. Extend the safety test to the breathing screen, and wait more than one phase before asserting 'suspended'.
