### AUD-045 - The design document has a literal '\n' in the Decision Log and a stale Document Control

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. Decision Log: "**confirmed by the owner\n  2026-10-07**)". This is a literal backslash-n, found by `grep -F 'owner\n'`. Document Control: "**Status:** signed off by the owner 2026-10-07; ready for Initialize." and "Version: 1.0". The Change Log ends at v1.0, although slice decisions were added through v0.5.0. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: This is a small loss of record quality. A reader cannot tell which design version matches which app version.
- Direction: Remove the escape. Update the Status and add Change Log rows for the slice amendments.
- Effort: S
