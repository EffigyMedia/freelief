### RLG-032 — Ripple pond: the click after a slow drag no longer adds a random ripple
- **RULED 2026-10-08T12:27:51-04:00** — Built in UNT-070 as v0.6.5. ripple.js replaces the 600 ms time window (config ripple.clickAfterPointerMs, removed) with a fromPointer flag: set on pointerdown, cleared by the click it causes, by a key press, or by pointercancel. Found when the suite ran on a slower machine: a 40-step drag took about 10 s, and its click added a random ripple. New test: a drag held for 900 ms leaves only its own ripples, and a key press after it still makes one. The trail test now counts ripples made and checks the cap with quick key presses, so it does not depend on the machine's speed. 136 tests.
