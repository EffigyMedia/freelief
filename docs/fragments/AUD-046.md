### AUD-046 - The reduced-motion guide scale is an in-code constant, not a config.json tunable

- Severity: Low
- Area: B. Code quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. exercises/breathe.js: `circle.style.transform = "scale(0.8)";`. The two other guide scales are tunables (`guideMinScale`, `guideMaxScale` in config.json). AGENTS.md: "Every tunable lives in `config.json` with a committed default — never an in-code constant." (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: The size of the still guide cannot be tuned like its other scales. This is a small rule breach.
- Direction: Add `guideStillScale` to config.json → breathing, and read it in breathe.js.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-010]].
- **RULED 2026-10-08T13:13:47-04:00** — Fixed in UNT-077: breathing.guideStillScale (0.8) in config.json replaces the in-code constant.
