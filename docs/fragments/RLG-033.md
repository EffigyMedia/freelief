### RLG-033 — Owner phone checks owed before 1.0
- **RULED 2026-10-08T13:16:35-04:00** — The checks the owner owes on a real phone before 1.0. The tests cannot observe look, feel, motion, sound or on-target speed, so a green test run does not verify these items (AGENTS.md). This item holds every pending owner check (AUD-017). Close it only when the owner reports each check; record each result here as a dated note.

Owed checks:
- On-phone launch time: a cold, offline launch of the installed app on a mid-range phone shows the menu within 1 second (REQ-026). bench measures only the throttled Playwright stand-in.
- Sort colors: drag a tile onto another tile and check that the two swap.
- Ripple pond: the ripples look and feel calm, and the muted drop sound is soft.
- Mandala coloring: choose a color and fill shapes; the shapes are large enough for a finger.
- The Visualizer: the softer rain, and the Both mode (music and rain together).
- The SVG logo on About: its look in the dark and the light theme.
- Urgent help: the Country list, the region saved in Settings, and the emergency Call buttons.
- The header sound button: it silences sound at once, mid-note, and turns it back on.
- Wake lock: the screen does not dim or lock during breathing or the Visualizer.
- About: the privacy text reads correctly.
- Settings, When Freelief opens: Start breathing at once opens on breathing.
- The quiet footer on an exercise or activity (only the calm line).
- The trace marker: about 44 px across, easy to hold with a finger.
- The rhythm line under the breathing circle (such as In 4 · Out 6).
- Feedback: Copy message copies the text, and it can be pasted into a GitHub issue.
- Vibration on Android: one short pulse for each single event in an activity, and nothing continuous.
- Portrait when installed: the installed app stays in portrait.
- Sort colors, manual screen-reader and keyboard check: owed under RLG-005's rule, which requires a manual accessibility check (AUD-015). The owner kept Sort colors in the menu on 2026-10-08; this check is still owed before 1.0.
