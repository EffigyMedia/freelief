### AUD-077 - The Effigy Media logo and the maker's name ship under the MIT license, and nothing reserves them as marks

- Severity: Low
- Area: E. Privacy & licensing
- Evidence: Quoted in this round at 82a1c6d (unchanged at b7964ce). screens/about.js draws the logo in shipped source: `<svg class="maker-logo" viewBox="0 0 197 94" role="img" aria-label="${t("about.logoAlt")}">` with its six `<rect>` bars, and strings/en.json has `"about.logoAlt": "Effigy Media"` and `"about.madeByName": "Alexander Steele, Effigy Media."`. LICENSE is the plain MIT grant to "deal in the Software without restriction", with no trademark reservation. No README or NOTICE file in the repository mentions marks (`git grep -i trademark` finds nothing). The owner's own policy, in <env-root>/TRADEMARKS.md, says: "'Effigy Media' and the Effigy Media logo are marks of Effigy Media. The open licenses ... grant no right to use them" and "Do not use the Effigy Media logo at all ... a fork should not carry it." (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: Freelief invites forks: it is public, MIT and has no build step. A fork, which may be broken or hostile, keeps an About page that shows the Effigy Media logo and "Freelief is made by Alexander Steele, Effigy Media", so it reads as made or endorsed by the owner. That is exactly the use the owner's mark policy forbids. Freelief is a crisis tool, so a bad fork under the owner's name and mark carries a reputational and safety cost. A note added later does not bind a fork already taken under the current text.
- Direction: Add a short trademark notice that is shipped with the source, in LICENSE or in a NOTICE file next to it. It should say that the names "Freelief" and "Effigy Media" and the Effigy Media logo are not licensed under MIT, and that a fork must remove the logo and the maker credit from About. Mention it in the public README. Ask the owner whether "Freelief" itself is reserved.
- Effort: S
