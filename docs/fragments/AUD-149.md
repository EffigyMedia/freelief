### AUD-149 - The changelog says an entry is never edited after the fact, but the deploy rule adds each move of live to an existing entry

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Process amendments
- Evidence: Quoted in this round. docs/core/changelog.md header: "**An entry is never edited after the fact.**" Design section 7 row 10: "Before 1.0, `live` moves only with the owner's yes, recorded in the changelog entry of the version it serves". Commit 2426fdd ('Record the owner's RLG-047 rulings and the move of live to v0.8.14') changed docs/core/changelog.md by 2 lines. It added "The live preview moved to 0.8.14 on 2026-10-10 ...; tag `preview-0.8.14`" to the [0.8.14] entry, after commit 4fac4ff had written that entry. (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: Two documented rules disagree, and each move of live breaks one of them. A reader cannot tell whether a changed entry is a lawful deploy record or an edit that rewrites history.
- Direction: State the exception in the changelog header ('a later line that records a move of live is the one permitted addition'), or record each move as a new dated line at the top. Then log the rule in the Instruction Changelog.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-124]].
