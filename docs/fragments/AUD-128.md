### AUD-128 - The design says the email route remains if GitHub is down, but the email route is hidden, so a volunteer with no GitHub account has no way to report

- Severity: Low
- Area: A. Design alignment
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-104. Design section 5, Integrations: "If GitHub is down, the issue page does not load and the email route remains." F9: "until the owner chooses a public email address, the email button is hidden and GitHub is the only route". config.json `project.feedbackEmail` is "", and screens/feedback.js renders the email choice `hidden` when it is empty. prd-users: "A community volunteer ... They want to report with little effort, through GitHub or email." Decision Log: "a pre-filled email for people with no GitHub account". (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: The volunteers who do the manual screen-reader checks that REQ-029 depends on are the people who may not have a GitHub account. Today nothing handles them, and the Integrations sentence tells a reader that a fallback exists. No standard can be shown as met without their check.
- Direction: Correct the Integrations sentence to match F9. Either name the owner's choice of address as the step that adds the fallback, or say that the page names no other route while email is hidden. Consider a no-account route that needs no server, for example a plain address shown as text once the owner chooses one.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-104]].
