### RLG-059 — Softer breathing sound: cut the highs a touch and lower the volume
- **RULED 2026-10-10T09:39:55-04:00** — 2026-10-10 (UNT-119, v0.8.10): built. A low-pass filter at sounds.breath.lowpassHz (2200 Hz) follows the whole breath sound, in, out and hold taps; the breath volume is 0.07 (was 0.09) and the tap volume 0.05 (was 0.06). test_the_breath_passes_through_a_soft_high_cut fails without the filter. How soft it sounds is an owner check in RLG-033.
