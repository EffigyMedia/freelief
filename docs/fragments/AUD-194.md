### AUD-194 - The rain always brings thunder, with no way to have rain without it, and the decision does not consider a person who fears storms

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (an actor that nothing handles); design concern for the owner
- Evidence: audio.js rain(): 'thunderTimer = setTimeout(roll, 1000 * thunder.firstAfterSeconds);'. This runs every time the rain plays. The sound bar has only 'music', 'rain' and 'waves'. Nothing about sound is stored, and Settings has no thunder choice. The Decision Log entry 'The rain has soft, distant thunder' has no Rejected alternatives and does not name fear of storms. The risk row 'A movement or a sound makes a person feel worse' gives the speaker button and 'sound waits while urgent help is open' as its mitigations, and it does not name the thunder. Design section 6: 'no surprise sound'. Confidence note: whether people need this choice is the owner's judgment, and the soft rise answers the startle concern. (quoted in round UNT-155) (quoted in round UNT-155)
- Impact: Fear of thunderstorms is a common anxiety trigger. A person who chooses rain to calm down hears a roll about 20 s later, and again every 40 to 90 s. The only way to avoid it is to give up the rain. The risk table tells a reader that the sound risks are covered, but this one is not weighed.
- Direction: Put one question to the owner. Either keep the thunder and add it to the risk row with the reason, or make it a separate choice, for example a long-press, a fourth sound-bar state, or rain without thunder by default. Record the ruling in the RLG-063 Decision Log entry.
