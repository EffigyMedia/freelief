### AUD-139 - The design's product overview, vocabulary and risk table still say Freelief claims standards and shows research, although RLG-056 removed both

- Severity: Medium
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in this round. The srs-product-overview region in design section 1 says: "Supporting pages give the self-help disclaimer, the standards Freelief meets with the research behind each technique, and a feedback page". docs/SRS.md repeats this: "Freelief meets with the research behind each technique". Section 4 table: "| **Standard** | An external standard Freelief claims to meet, such as WCAG 2.2 AA. It has a level, a check date, and the tester of the manual check. |". The srs-definitions region and docs/SRS.md say: "**Standard** — an external standard Freelief claims, with its level, check date and tester." Section 4 Relations says: "each Exercise and Activity cites one or more Sources" (REQ-024 is withdrawn). Section 11 risk row (omission lens): "each technique cites its research (REQ-024)" (withdrawn). Against these: AGENTS.md says "The app makes no standards claim and has no research page (owner, 2026-10-10, RLG-056)", and the Decision Log entry "No Standards and research page, and no standards claim" agrees. The AUD-126 close (UNT-118) said: "The clauses that still named the removed Standards page (references, success criteria, scope, F8, the module table, slice 4, ...) are corrected too. SRS and PRD regenerated." That close missed these clauses. (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: The generated SRS is a public specification, and it tells a reader that Freelief meets standards and shows research. The owner withdrew that claim because most of the research is limited or indirect, so that the app makes no standards claim. A later session that reads the vocabulary can build a standards claim again.
- Direction: Rewrite the srs-product-overview sentence without the standards and research page. Mark the Standard term in section 4 and in srs-definitions as withdrawn, or remove it with a note, and change the Source relation to say that the sources are a record in docs/research/sources.md. Fix the REQ-024 risk row. Regenerate SRS.md and PRD.md. Then grep the design for 'claims', 'Standard' and 'research behind' to find any other clause.
- Effort: S
