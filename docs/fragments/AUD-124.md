### AUD-124 - The shipped size is at 95% of its 250 KB limit before slice 8 closes, and nothing plans the next additions against the 11.4 KB left

- Severity: Low
- Area: D. Performance and resources
- Evidence: The health check build printed 'Shipped: 35 file(s), 238.6 KB of 250 KB (REQ-028)'. bench, round UNT-104: '[FLAG] Shipped size (REQ-028): 238.6 KB ... baseline 188.7 KB, +26%'. log.md v0.7.11: '232.8 KB ... 17 KB of budget left'. The size grew 5.8 KB between v0.7.11 and v0.7.13, and about 50 KB in slice 8. The largest files are styles.css 26.7 KB, icons/icon-512.png 26.1 KB, app.js 22.6 KB, audio.js 21.9 KB, strings/en.json 18.3 KB and config.json 15.2 KB. (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: The next feature of slice 8's size breaks `build` at release time, under time pressure. The likely quick fix is then to cut text or icons, not to make a planned choice. Users are not hurt now.
- Direction: At the slice 8 checkpoint, record the size in the log. Add a size plan to the design: what each slice may spend, and which known savings exist, such as minified CSS, a smaller 512 px PNG, or config groups that only tools read. The owner can then decide before the limit is hit.
