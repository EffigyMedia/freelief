### AUD-015 - The colour sort shipped under a rule that dropped the manual accessibility check that RLG-005 requires

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Design alignment
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. RLG-005 ruling: "It ships only if it passes the automated and manual accessibility checks; otherwise it stays out of the release." The RLG-005 close says: "It passed axe in both themes and a keyboard-only solve, so it ships under REQ-014's rule." The Decision Log (slice 5) says: "It passed every automated check, so under REQ-014's rule it ships." REQ-014's text has no such rule. The keyboard-only solve is an automated Playwright test (test_the_sort_can_be_solved_by_keyboard_alone). No record shows a manual screen-reader check. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: Screen-reader users on the live site meet an activity that no person has checked. The rule that made the colour sort conditional was weakened without the owner's decision, and the record names a rule that does not exist.
- Direction: Record a manual screen-reader and keyboard check of the colour sort, or hide it from the menu until that check exists. Correct the Decision Log and RLG-005 so that they cite RLG-005's rule as it is written.
- Effort: S
