### REQ-014 — Freelief offers a colour sort: the person puts calm colours into order from lightest to darkest by choosing a tile and then the tile to swap it with, by touch, mouse or keyboard, or by dragging a tile onto another tile by touch or mouse, with no score and no failure.

**History.**
- 2026-10-07 (agreed in the design interview): "...into gradient order by drag or by keyboard...".
- 2026-10-07 (slice 5, UNT-008, implementer): changed to choose-then-swap. One method then serves
  touch, mouse, keyboard and screen reader alike, with no hidden second interaction to learn, and
  each tile names its shade ("Blue, very light") so the task works without sight. Drag was not
  built. **This change waits for the owner's confirmation**; if the owner wants drag as well, it is
  an addition, not a replacement.
- 2026-10-07 (owner, UNT-029): the owner confirmed choose-then-swap and asked for drag as an
  addition. Drag was added: a drop on another tile swaps the two. The text above now names both
  methods. This resolves the open confirmation that AUD-016 found.
- **RULED 2026-10-09T08:24:58-04:00** — 2026-10-09 (owner, RLG-040; UNT-096): withdrawn. Unblock replaces Sort colors; its requirement is REQ-035. The owner rejected a falling-block game and chose Unblock from three rounds of proposals.
