### AUD-187 - The background sound's tunables are split between the Kaleidoscope's config group and sounds.background

- Severity: Low
- Area: B. Architecture & code quality (one owner per concern; naming against the domain vocabulary)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-155. config.json `calm` holds 'bothMix': {'music': 0.9, 'nature': 0.6} and 'duckRoutes': ['breathe'] beside 'kaleidoscope'. `sounds.background` holds {'duckLevel': 0.35, 'duckSeconds': 1.2}. background.js: 'const musicLevel = () => (nature ? config.calm.bothMix.music : 1);'. app.js: 'audio.duck(config.calm.duckRoutes.includes(name));'. Design section 4: the Kaleidoscope 'has no sound choice of its own; the sound bar plays sound on every screen'. Module table: background.js owns 'The background sound ... The shell's sound bar tells it what to play'. The tunables line documents the split: '`calm` (the Kaleidoscope, and the mix and ducking of the background sound)'. (quoted in round UNT-155)
- Impact: This is a leftover from the time when Calm owned the sound (RLG-045, RLG-049). A maintainer who tunes the ducking must edit two groups, and one of them is named for a screen that plays no sound. The next sweep of the Kaleidoscope (the AUD-143 rule) could move or drop the `calm` group and silently break the background mix. People are not hurt now.
- Direction: Move `bothMix` and `duckRoutes` into `sounds.background`, beside `duckLevel` and `duckSeconds`. Update background.js, app.js and the tests that read them. Correct the design's tunables line and the exercises_and_settings.md reference. Keep `calm` for the Kaleidoscope only.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-155]].
