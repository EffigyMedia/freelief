### AUD-152 - The automated accessibility check runs only on each screen's opening state, not on 'every page and state' as the design promises

- Severity: Medium
- Area: H. Test coverage
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-124. Design section 9, Tests: '*Accessibility:* axe-core on every page and state, with zero A and AA violations; a keyboard-only walk through every flow.' tools/tests/test_a11y.py is the only file that runs axe (a grep for Axe/_violations in tools/tests finds no other use). Its _check() sets `location.hash` to each name in SCREENS, runs axe once, then opens '.help-open' and runs axe once on the dialog, in each theme. No other state is checked: Unblock's Slide buttons ('<div class="unblock-slide" hidden>' until a block is chosen), a chosen Zen Garden item (an SVG '<g ... role: "button", "aria-pressed">'), the Kaleidoscope's full screen and black cover, Settings' reset question ('<div class="reset-confirm" hidden>'), Feedback's 'problem' kind and its copy-failed state, a solved Unblock board (inert blue block), and the help dialog for a region other than the en-US default or for 'Another country'. (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: A screen-reader or keyboard user meets these states in normal use, and keyboard and screen-reader use is a never-break path. A violation that appears only after a tap (for example a control in the Slide bar, the full-screen help button, or the reset question) ships with a green run. The design's test list tells a reader that this coverage exists.
- Direction: Extend test_a11y.py with a list of states, each a short script that reaches the state and then runs axe in both themes: Unblock with a block chosen and with the board solved; Zen Garden with an item chosen; the Kaleidoscope in full screen and under the black cover; Settings with the reset question open; Feedback with 'A problem or an idea' chosen; the help dialog on each curated region and on 'Another country'. Or change design section 9 to say exactly which states are checked.
- Effort: M
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-124]].
