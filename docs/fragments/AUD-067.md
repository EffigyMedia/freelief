### AUD-067 - About's privacy text and the design's definition of Settings list three stored items, but Freelief stores six, including the chosen region

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Design alignment
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. settings.js stores the defaults `rhythm`, `sounds`, `theme`, `calmMode`, `helpRegion` and `haptics`. strings/en.json "about.privacy2": "Your settings (breathing rhythm, sounds and colors) are saved only on this device." Design Stage 4: "**Settings** | The only stored data: the chosen rhythm, tones on or off, and the light or dark choice if overridden." (also "Settings — the only data Freelief stores: rhythm, tones, and theme"). F7: "Rhythm preset, tones on or off, theme override." Stage 8 Data: "Nothing is created, changed or deleted by the person except their Settings." The settings screen header comment names the region, and nav.settingsHint says "Breathing rhythm, sounds and colors." (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The privacy statement is a user-facing claim under the 'true claims only' principle, on a trust page, and it does not list everything that is kept. It omits the saved country for urgent help, which is the most personal item. The design's own definition of 'the only stored data', which REQ-015 relies on, is out of date, so a later privacy review that works from the design misses three keys.
- Direction: Update about.privacy2 to list everything stored (rhythm, sounds, vibration, colors, the Visualizer sound and the country for urgent help), or name the categories so that the text stays true. Update Stage 4, the srs-definitions region and F7. Add a test that compares the keys in settings.js defaults with a list kept beside the privacy text, or generate the list from one place.
