### RLG-060 — Zen Garden: the raking sound is one continuous loop whose loudness follows the rake's speed

A loop made while sound was silent is made again on the next stroke. garden.soundEveryMs and sounds.sand.seconds are removed. test_raking_plays_one_continuous_sound_that_follows_the_speed fails against the old code. How it sounds is an owner check in RLG-033.
