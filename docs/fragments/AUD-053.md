### AUD-053 - No stated handling exists for a feedback issue from a person who is in danger

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (actor with no handler)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. Design section 2 gives the owner only: "maintains the crisis-line list, accepts volunteer checks, and releases versions." Neither issue template points to urgent help. The problem-report template asks "What happened, or what would help:". AGENTS.md and the design give no rule for triage or response to a crisis disclosure in an issue. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: A distressed person can use Feedback as a call for help. The owner has no planned response, and the person gets no pointer to a crisis line on the page where they wrote.
- Direction: Add one line to both templates and to the in-app templates: 'If you are in danger now, use Need urgent help or call your local emergency number. Issues are not watched around the clock.' Record in the design how the owner responds to such an issue.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-010]].
- **RULED 2026-10-08T12:39:11-04:00** — Partly fixed in UNT-072 as v0.6.7: the Feedback page shows a visible line that feedback is not watched around the clock and points to Need urgent help or the local emergency number; both templates carry it as a comment. Still open: the owner's response rule for an issue from a person in danger, to be stated in the design.
