### AUD-020 - REQ-030 (must) requires the email route, but the design allows a release with the email button hidden

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Requirement compliance
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. REQ-030, priority must: "A feedback page opens a pre-filled GitHub issue ... and, as a second choice, a pre-filled email to the public project address." Design Stage 11 open question: "The owner decides, before the first release that shows the email button. Until then the button is hidden." Stage 10 release criteria: "every 'must' requirement is met". config.json: "feedbackEmail": "". (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: The design contradicts itself. The 1.0 release either breaches a must requirement or is blocked by a question the design treats as optional. People with no GitHub account have no feedback route.
- Direction: The owner either chooses the address before release, or edits REQ-030 with its history to make email conditional. The open-question wording then follows that ruling.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-010]].
