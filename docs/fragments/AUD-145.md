### AUD-145 - The removed trace survives in design section 8 and in two shipped tunables that nothing reads, and the design names no module for Zen Garden

- Severity: Low
- Area: B. Dead code
- Evidence: Quoted in this round. Design section 8 module table: "`activities/bubbles.js`, `trace.js`, `unblock.js`, `ripple.js`, `mandala.js` | One activity each." There is no row for activities/garden.js, which ships, so Zen Garden has no boundary or 'Must not' (omission lens). The Tunables paragraph says: "`bubbles`, `trace`, `unblock` (the boards)". config.json has a 'garden' group and no 'trace' group. The haptics.js row says: "Vibrate for anything continuous (the breath, a drag trail, the trace tone)". config.json still ships sounds.cues.loop ({'notes': [392.0, 523.25], ...}) and haptics.patterns has 'loop'. The 2026-10-07 vibration entry says these were for "a finished loop" of the trace. git grep finds no audio.play("loop") and no haptic("loop"). The only calls are play("choose"), play("swap") and play("done"). (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: A reader of section 8 looks for a trace.js that does not exist and finds no boundary or 'Must not' for Zen Garden's module, and no Zen Garden tunables. Two unused tunables ship in a budget that has about 17 KB left (233.1 of 250 KB), and the design's size plan lists 'config groups that only the tools read' as a saving.
- Direction: In section 8, replace trace.js with garden.js, with its own 'Must not', replace `trace` with `garden` in the tunables list, and remove 'the trace tone' from the haptics row. Remove sounds.cues.loop and haptics.patterns.loop from config.json after a check that no code or test reads them. Bump the patch version, because config.json ships. Regenerate SRS and PRD if a marked region changes.
