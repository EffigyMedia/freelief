### AUD-048 - No record shows the look variants that the design says the implementer shows the owner

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Missing documentation
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. Design Stage 6: "The implementer shows the owner variants before the look is fixed." A search for 'variant' across every .md file, the fragments and core documents finds only the design sentence. The look shipped publicly at v0.5.0. The untracked tools/icon_drafts.py ("Draw draft app icons ... for the owner to choose from") shows that icon variants are only now in progress. Confidence note: the variants may have been shown in chat and not recorded. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: The visual look of a calm-first app is live on a public URL with no recorded owner choice. A later session cannot tell whether the choice was made.
- Direction: Record the owner's choice of look as a Decision Log entry, or schedule the variant review as a tracker item (or add it to the open owner-check item) and show the variants (tools/previews.py exists for this).
- Effort: S
