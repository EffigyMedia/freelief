### RLG-027 — Safety: tappable emergency number, silence during help and in the background, wake lock, help in full screen, nothing drawn under the black screen
- **RULED 2026-10-08T01:48:00-04:00** — Built in UNT-054 as v0.5.39. Emergency Call buttons parsed from the region's emergency text (tel: per number; IE gives 112 and 999; none for Another country). Audio suspends on help open and on page hidden, resumes on close or visible only when sound is on and help is closed. wakelock.js keepAwake() (Screen Wake Lock API, re-acquired on visibility) used by breathe.js and calm.js and released in stop(). Visualizer full screen shows Need urgent help? beside Leave full screen; it leaves full screen and opens the dialog. Under the black screen addShape adds nothing and the stage is .asleep (field hidden, animations paused). test_safety.py: five tests; two mutation checks (no silence on help open; shapes under black) made their tests fail. 118 tests. How the wake lock and the battery saving behave on a real phone is for the owner to check.
