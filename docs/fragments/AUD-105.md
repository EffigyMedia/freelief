### AUD-105 - There is no incident path for a defect in the live release: the design's rollback is forbidden by the rule that live moves only at a gated release

- Severity: Medium
- Area: H. Build & deployment readiness
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082. Design section 7, row 7: "Rollback moves `live` back to an earlier commit." Same row and AGENTS.md: "from 1.0 on, it moves only at a release, after `audit-gate.py` prints `GATE CLEAR`, to a tag". A gate round needs four lenses on the strongest model, and no open finding. No document says who may move `live` back, to what, how fast, or how installed copies get the rollback. The words hotfix, incident and emergency do not appear. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: After 1.0, a wrong crisis number or a broken start-up found in the field has two options. The owner breaks the stated rule, or waits for a full release round while users get the defect. The most dangerous field defect has the slowest path.
- Direction: Write an incident procedure. State what allows moving `live` back to the previous release tag with no new round, who decides, and what is recorded. Confirm that a rollback reaches installed copies: the older version.js gives a different sw.js, so a new cache installs and takes over at the next open. Say whether a fix-forward release may run a scoped round.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-082]].
