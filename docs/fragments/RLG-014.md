### RLG-014 — Mandala coloring activity: tap a part to fill it with a soft color
- **RULED 2026-10-07T23:54:02-04:00** — Built in UNT-040 as v0.5.26. activities/mandala.js draws three designs from config.json mandala.designs (rings of petal, band or dot shapes around a center) as SVG paths; six soft swatches (mandala.palette) are a radio group whose colors are set from script, because the CSP refuses inline style attributes. Each shape is role button with a name, one tab stop with arrow keys (left/right around a ring, up/down between rings, Home/End), Enter or Space fills; a polite status line repeats the name; the step cue plays. Inner petals were widened after the size test found 21.9 px in design 3. test_mandala.py: tap fill and refill, names and one tab stop, keyboard-only coloring with wrap, New mandala, every shape >= 24 px at 360 px and swatches >= 44 px, the cue. 102 tests; 163.6 KB; bench 134 ms, 58 ms. Look and feel is for the owner to check on a phone.
