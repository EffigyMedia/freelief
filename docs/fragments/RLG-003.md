### RLG-003 — Slice 3 - distraction: bubble field and shape trace
- **RULED 2026-10-07T13:15:59-04:00** — Slice 3 adds the distraction activities that must ship: the bubble field and the shape trace. Both use DOM elements, with no score, no failure and no timer, and both work by touch, keyboard and screen reader.
- **RULED 2026-10-07T13:45:43-04:00** — Built in UNT-006 as v0.3.0: the bubble field (grid slots so no bubble covers another, a stop-the-drifting control for WCAG 2.2.2, still under reduced motion, focus kept in the field after a pop) and the shape trace (a figure eight inside a role=slider, arrow keys and pointer, loops noted without a score). Three distraction sources checked on 2026-10-07, with the safety-behaviour caveat recorded in the design document. 33 tests pass, the random-placement tests four times in a row; bench 124 ms, 9 ms, 70 KB. Still open: the feel of the drift and the trace on a phone, for the owner.
