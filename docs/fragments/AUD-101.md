### AUD-101 - REQ-017 gained a second exception without an owner acceptance or a Decision Log entry, and two places still say there is one exception

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082 at 82a1c6d. REQ-017's history: "2026-10-08 (UNT-077, AUD-039): a second documented exception. The crisis-line names, the country names and the hours in `data/crisis-lines.json` stay in that file". The first exception says "**The owner accepted this exception on 2026-10-07.**", and the second has no acceptance. The requirement text still says "All user-facing text lives in one strings file per language". The design's Decision Log still says "The fallback text is the one exception to REQ-017", and the index.html comment says "Its text is the one exception to REQ-017". No Decision Log entry records the second exception. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: A rule that the owner made, with an exception that the owner accepted, now has a second exception that the implementer chose. Two documents still say there is only one. A translator or auditor who works from REQ-017 and the design does not know that crisis-line text needs a translated data file.
- Direction: Put the second exception to the owner. If accepted, record the acceptance in REQ-017's history, add a Decision Log entry, and change 'the one exception' in the Decision Log and in index.html to name both exceptions. Regenerate the SRS.
