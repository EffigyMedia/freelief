### AUD-165 - A screen that throws during start is dropped without stop(), so its wake lock, timers or sounds stay alive

- Severity: Low
- Area: C. Error handling
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-139. app.js `show()`: `current = screen;` then, in the catch of `await current.start(...)`: `console.error(...); if (request !== showing) return; current = null;` and then it falls back to the menu. `screen.stop()` is never called. breathe.js and calm.js take `ctx.keepAwake()` at the top of start(), before the rest of their set-up. (quoted in round UNT-139)
- Impact: If a start fails after its first lines run, the screen stays on (the wake lock), and timers or audio from the broken screen continue under the menu until the page closes. The cause is rare: a malformed config value. But this is the exact failure path the fallback was built for.
- Direction: In the catch, call `screen.stop?.()` inside its own try/catch before `current = null`. Extend test_a_failed_screen_start_falls_back_to_the_menu_and_links_still_work with a stub whose start takes ctx.keepAwake() and then throws, and assert that the lock is released.
- Effort: S
