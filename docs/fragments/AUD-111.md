### AUD-111 - No plan covers moving or retiring the deployment, and installed copies would keep their frozen crisis lines with no notice

- Severity: Low
- Area: H. Build & deployment readiness
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082. Design section 9: "an origin of its own (a custom domain or a dedicated account) removes this dependency". Section 7 names GitHub Pages as the host. sw.js answers from its cache first: `caches.open(CACHE).then((cache) => cache.match(...))`. No clause says how installed copies learn about a new origin, or what happens to them if the site or the repository goes away. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: If the owner moves Freelief to its own origin, as the design suggests, or retires it, every installed copy keeps serving its last cached version offline, crisis lines included, with nothing to tell the person.
- Direction: Write the end-of-life and origin-move steps. For example, keep a final worker at the old origin that tells the person about the new address, or that unregisters after a stated period. Reference Process/Teardown_Policy.md.
- Effort: M
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-082]].
