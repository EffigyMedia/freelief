### REQ-018 — When Freelief opens, it shows the menu of exercises and activities at once, with no account, sign-up, notice or question before it; the person can choose in Settings to open on breathing instead. Breathing is the first item, one tap away, and every screen has a Back to menu control.

**History.**
- 2026-10-07 (agreed in the design interview): "When Freelief opens, the paced breathing guide
  starts at once, with no account, sign-up, menu or question before it."
- 2026-10-07 (owner, UNT-022): changed. The owner, testing on the phone, asked to "open to the main
  menu instead of the breathing. and each goes back to menu".
- 2026-10-08 (owner, UNT-061): the design review asked for a zero- or one-tap path to breathing.
  The owner chose the polish group: the Breathe card stands out, and Settings has "When Freelief
  opens: Show the menu / Start breathing at once". The menu stays the default.
