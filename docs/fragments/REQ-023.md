### REQ-023 — The person can choose a breathing rhythm from presets (4-in 6-out by default, a slower rhythm, and box breathing), and the choice is remembered on the device.

**History.**
- 2026-10-07 (agreed in the design interview): "(4-in 6-out by default, a slower rhythm, and box
  breathing)".
- 2026-10-07 (owner, UNT-031): box breathing (4-4-4-4) is the default and is listed first. A device
  that already saved a rhythm keeps it.
- 2026-10-08 (owner, UNT-050): back to 4-in 6-out as the default, listed first. The source check
  of 2026-10-08 found that in Balban 2023 an exhale-focused method gave a larger gain than box
  breathing; the owner asked for the longer breath out as the default if it does better.
