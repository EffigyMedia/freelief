### RLG-063 — Rain: soft, low, distant thunder now and then
- **RULED 2026-10-10T17:21:28-04:00** — 2026-10-10 (UNT-144, v0.8.27): built. audio.rain() schedules thunder (sounds.rain.thunder): low-passed looping noise under cutoffHz 160, level 3.0 inside the rain's master, a 0.9 s linear rise, two or three softer swells, an exponential fade over 6 to 9 s; first after 20 s, then every 40 to 90 s; stopped with the rain. No crack, by design: nothing may start suddenly. test_the_rain_brings_soft_low_thunder_that_never_starts_suddenly fails against the old code.
