### AUD-061 - The trust page and the default-rhythm decision say that a longer out-breath did better than box breathing in Balban 2023, but the trial did not test that comparison, and 4-in 6-out was not one of its arms

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Design alignment
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. strings/en.json "standards.evidence.breathing": "In one trial, breathing with a longer breath out gave the largest gain, more than box breathing, so Freelief starts with 4 in and 6 out." The Decision Log (UNT-050) says: "in Balban 2023 an exhale-focused method gave a larger mood gain than box breathing, and the owner asked for the longer breath out if it does better." changelog 0.5.36: "in a trial, a longer breath out did better than box breathing." The project's own research record, docs/research/sources.md, says only: "The exhale-focused practice (cyclic sighing) improved mood and lowered respiratory rate more than mindfulness meditation. Box breathing was one of the arms." This round read the paper (PMC9873947). Its abstract compares the methods with mindfulness only: "breathwork, especially the exhale-focused cyclic sighing, produces greater improvement in mood (p < 0.05) and reduction in respiratory rate (p < 0.05) compared with mindfulness meditation." The results say: "The other two breathwork groups were also higher than mindfulness meditation; however, this difference was not significant." No significance test between cyclic sighing and box breathing is reported. Cyclic sighing is a double in-breath and a long out-breath, not 4 in and 6 out. Confidence note: the paper was read through a fetched summary of the PMC full text, not page by page. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The owner made the change on a condition ("if it does better"), and the evidence on record does not meet it. The Standards and research page exists to state evidence honestly, and here it states a comparison the trial did not test. It then makes that comparison the reason for a preset that the trial never studied. This weakens design principle 4 ("True claims only") on the page that people use to judge whether to trust Freelief. It does not harm safety, because every preset is a slow, gentle rhythm.
- Direction: Give the owner the corrected reading of Balban 2023: the breathwork arms were not compared with each other for significance, only the point estimate of cyclic sighing was highest, and 4-in 6-out was not tested. The owner then rules again on the default. Keep 4-in 6-out on other grounds (slow breathing under 10 breaths a minute, per Zaccaro 2018, and the original interview decision), or choose another default. Reword standards.evidence.breathing so that it says only what the trial showed. Correct the REQ-023 history and the Decision Log annotation in place, with a dated note. Update the Balban entry in sources.md. The changelog is append-only, so a later entry states the correction.
- Effort: S
