### RLG-015 — Visualizer: softer rain, and a well-mixed option with both music and nature sound
- **RULED 2026-10-07T23:18:50-04:00** — Built in UNT-035 as v0.5.22. Rain: volume 0.035 (was 0.05), low-pass 1800 Hz (2600), high-pass 300 Hz (350), drops at volume 0.035 (0.07), 1700 Hz (2400), every 300 ms (260), with a 90 ms decay and Q 4 that are now tunables (dropSeconds, dropQ; were 60 ms and 6 in code). Both: calm.modes gains 'both'; pads(level) and rain(level) take a level, and Both plays them at calm.bothMix 0.9 and 0.6. Test: Both is remembered, leaving stops the pads and the rain, and a fresh start in Both plays the triangle-wave pads and the looping rain together. Proven: the test fails with the pads alone and with the rain alone. 89 tests. How soft the rain sounds and how well the mix sits are for the owner to judge on a phone.
