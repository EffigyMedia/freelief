### AUD-018 - The colour sort is missing from the Standards and research page and sources.md, and nothing maps an activity to a technique

- Severity: Medium
- Area: A. Requirement compliance
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. strings/en.json: "standards.technique.distraction": "Bubbles and tracing". data/research.json techniques are breathing, grounding, statements and distraction. docs/research/sources.md heading: "## Distraction activities (bubble field, shape trace)". Curry & Kasser 2005 is described there as "It supports a structured, repetitive visual task, such as tracing a loop; it did not test bubbles or tracing." Design Stage 4: "each Exercise and Activity cites one or more Sources." REQ-024 (must): "the Standards & research page cites at least one source for each technique." test_every_technique_cites_dated_sources checks only the techniques that research.json lists. It does not check that every route in the menu belongs to a technique. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: The page whose job is honest evidence leaves out a shipped activity. A reader cannot tell what the colour sort rests on, and the Stage 4 relation is broken. The next activity that is added will repeat the gap, and no test will fail.
- Direction: Add the colour sort to the distraction technique on the Standards page (for example 'Bubbles, tracing and colour sorting'), in strings and research.json, and to sources.md, with a source and an honest strength note, or a statement that no source tested it; decide whether curry2005 covers it. Add a map from each exercise and activity route to a technique id, and a test that every menu route maps to a technique with sources.
- Effort: S
