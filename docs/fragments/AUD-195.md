### AUD-195 - REQ-038 still requires lines that 'flow around' the stones and plants, but the owner's ruling RLG-061 removed the rings, and the requirement has no history line

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (a changed rule whose requirement was not edited)
- Evidence: REQ-038 (agreed): '... adds, moves and removes a few stones and small plants, each named for a screen reader, with lines that flow around them and a way to smooth the sand ...'. Its only history line is the agreement of 2026-10-10 (UNT-115). RLG-061 (built, UNT-142, v0.8.25): 'garden.js draws no rings: the .garden-rings layer, its CSS and garden.rings and ringGap are gone; the mask hides raked lines only under each item'. Design F5: 'nothing is drawn around them, and the raked lines stop at their edges (owner, RLG-061)'. AGENTS.md: 'A changed requirement is edited in its own `REQ-` record with its history beneath it'. Confidence note: 'flow around' could be read as the person raking around the items. The ruling's title, 'no rings drawn around them', suggests that it meant the rings. - Impact: The generated SRS states a behavior that the app no longer has, so the agreed requirement and the design disagree. A later reader or a test written from REQ-038 could put the rings back.
