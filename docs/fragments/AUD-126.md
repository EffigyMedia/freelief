### AUD-126 - The design's index clauses were not updated for slice 8: Document Control names version 1.3 with no Change Log entry, section 5 stops at REQ-034, the risk table still has the colour sort, and code comments drift the same way

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-104. Section 0: "Version: 1.3, 2026-10-09. The Change Log (section 15) says what each version changed." Section 15 ends at "v1.2 — 2026-10-08", with no v1.3 entry. Section 5: "The requirements are the records `REQ-001` to `REQ-034`. Their priority is the feature list." The store holds REQ-035 (Unblock), REQ-036 (the Visualizer) and REQ-037 (the wake lock), all agreed. Section 11 Risks: "| The colour sort cannot be made fully accessible. | It is \"should\", not \"must\", and built last; it ships only when it passes. |" REQ-014 is withdrawn, F5 says Unblock replaced it, and there is no risk row for Unblock. AUD-095 removed the app-version pin and pointed Document Control at the Change Log; the Change Log was not then kept. Code comments drift the same way. screens/settings.js header: "Settings: breathing rhythm, the nature sound, colors, vibration and the region for urgent help" (it omits where it opens, the screen-on time and Reset). styles.css: "the colour sort's shades, the mandala's fills and its swatches ARE the content". (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: A reader cannot find what version 1.3 changed. A reader who takes section 5 as the feature list misses three agreed requirements. The risk table tracks a feature that no longer exists, and it has no row for the activity that replaced it or for the new risks of slice 8, such as background sound that persists across screens. Each clause disagrees with the records that it summarises. The impact is small but repeats the drift that AUD-095 recorded.
- Direction: Add the v1.3 Change Log entry for slice 8. Change section 5 to cite the range that the generators report, or to say "every agreed REQ- record" with no range, or update it to REQ-037. Replace the colour-sort risk row with the Unblock and background-sound risks, and mark the old row as superseded or withdrawn. Correct the two comments. Consider a test_repo check that the version in section 0 has a Change Log entry.
