### AUD-188 - The performance log entry and the AUD-158 ruling for the v0.8.32 move state two different shipped sizes

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-155. docs/performance/log.md, entry '2026-10-10 — Live move to v0.8.32': the table row is '| Shipped size | 239.8 KB | 235.9 KB | -2% | OK |', and the Notes in the same entry say 'Size 234.3 KB after the palette icons.' The AUD-158 ruling in a6dc105 says 'size 234.3 KB'. 234.3 KB is the v0.8.30 figure ('Shipped 245.8 to 234.3 KB', AUD-124). The health step's `build` printed 'Shipped: 32 file(s), 235.9 KB of 250 KB', and output/bench-log-draft.md for v0.8.32 has '235.9 KB'. (quoted in round UNT-155)
- Impact: The log is the record of size history (AUD-135), and the size plan's next decision depends on it. One entry gives two numbers 1.6 KB apart, and a later reader cannot tell which is right without running build again. The effect is small, but this log exists so that a regression is found at the version that caused it.
- Direction: Correct the Notes of the v0.8.32 log entry and append a correction to AUD-158 that gives 235.9 KB, with 1.6 KB added after the palette icons by v0.8.31 and v0.8.32. Write log notes from the draft that bench produces, not from an earlier entry.
