### RLG-034 — Sound button fades the sound out and in, not a hard cut
- **RULED 2026-10-08T13:19:19-04:00** — Built in UNT-078 as v0.6.13. audio.js routes every sound through one master GainNode; applySound(false) ramps it to 0 over sounds.muteFadeSeconds (0.6 s) and then suspends the context; applySound(true) resumes and ramps back to 1, and a quick off-then-on cancels the pending pause. Urgent help and a hidden page use the same fade. Test: right after the button press the audio still runs (no hard cut), then it pauses, then on resumes; it fails against an immediate suspend. The help test now waits for the fade. How the fade feels is for the owner to check on a phone.
