### RLG-036 — Mandala: a rain-chime note for each color, when chosen and when filled
- **RULED 2026-10-08T21:55:14-04:00** — Built in UNT-084 as v0.7.1: audio.chime(frequency) strikes four inharmonic sine partials (1, 2.76, 5.4, 8.93) with their own decays and a 0.35-level second strike after 0.11 s (sounds.chime); mandala.palette gives each color a pentatonic note (523.25 to 1046.5 Hz); choosing a swatch and filling a shape play it. The unused step cue is removed. Test: two colors each sound their own note when chosen and when filled; no two colors share a note. How the chime sounds is for the owner to judge on a phone.
