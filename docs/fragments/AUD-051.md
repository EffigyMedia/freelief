### AUD-051 - The design does not say what happens when the phone screen sleeps during breathing

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (design concern)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. Design F1 lists the states first visit, offline, reduced motion and storage blocked. Section 2: "The context is often a phone at night". No file uses the Screen Wake Lock API, and the design does not mention screen sleep. This is a design concern, not a confirmed defect. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: On many phones the screen dims or locks within 30 seconds while the person only watches the breathing guide. They must unlock the phone with shaky hands, which works against 'help first'.
- Direction: Decide with the owner whether the breathing screen requests a screen wake lock while it runs (and releases it on pause or when the screen changes), and record the decision in F1.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-010]].
