### AUD-080 - The offline copy is best-effort storage: Freelief never asks for persistent storage, so the browser can evict it and the app then will not open offline

- Severity: Medium
- Area: C. Edge cases (resource exhaustion)
- Evidence: Round UNT-082: `grep -rn "persist\|storage.estimate" --include=*.js .` finds no call in any shipped file. The design document names no eviction risk: grep for 'evict' and 'persist' finds only the Settings row 'Persistence | localStorage for Settings only'. sw.js keeps the whole app in Cache Storage under `freelief-<version>`. The only repair is 'on each online launch the page asks the worker to fetch any missing file ("heal")', which app.js gates with `if (!navigator.onLine) return;`. The origin effigymedia.github.io is shared with other apps (sw.js header, AUD-001), so their storage counts against the same origin quota. Confidence note: eviction was not reproduced here. It is the platform behavior for best-effort storage under storage pressure, and Safari's cap applies to script-written storage of sites that are not installed. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: This hurts a person on a low-storage phone, or one who uses Freelief in the browser without installing it. They open it offline in a panic and get the browser's network error page, not the breathing guide or the urgent-help control. Nothing warns them in advance. REQ-008's promise ('works with no network') then fails at the moment the app exists for.
- Direction: Decide and record this in the design (Decision Log). Call `navigator.storage.persist()` where the browser grants it without a prompt (Chromium grants it silently to installed or engaged sites). Do not call it where it shows a permission prompt (Firefox), because the launch must have no question or notice. Optionally show the persisted state on About or Settings. Add a test that the call is made and that it does not block start-up.
