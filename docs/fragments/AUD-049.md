### AUD-049 - Saved 'tones on' plays nothing at launch until the person taps, and nothing says so

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (unhandled state)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. audio.js: "Call from a user gesture (turning tones on), so the browser allows sound." At launch, breathe.js calls ctx.audio.cue with no gesture, and ensureContext does "if (context.state === \"suspended\") context.resume();". test_tones_stay_silent_by_default_and_play_when_on counts createOscillator calls, so it cannot hear a suspended context. Confidence: from reading the code and the browser autoplay rules. This was not observed on a device. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: A person who chose tones hears silence in the zero-tap launch flow, until they touch something. This can confuse someone who relies on the sound.
- Direction: Decide the behaviour: for example, show a quiet 'Tap to hear tones' hint when tones are on and the context is suspended, or document that tones start at the first touch. Add the state to design F7.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-010]].
