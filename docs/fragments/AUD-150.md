### AUD-150 - AGENTS.md says the REQ- records are all agreed, but seven are withdrawn

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in this round. AGENTS.md: "`docs/fragments/` — the store. `REQ-` records are the requirements (all agreed)". A grep of the store's statuses: 31 agreed and 7 withdrawn (REQ-002, REQ-003, REQ-013, REQ-014, REQ-020, REQ-024, REQ-029). The SRS header says "31 requirement(s) specified, 7 not yet". (quoted in round UNT-124)
- Impact: A session that trusts AGENTS.md can treat a withdrawn REQ- record, such as REQ-020 (the Standards page) or REQ-013 (the trace), as a live requirement and build it again.
- Direction: Change the clause to say that agreed REQ- records are the requirements, and that withdrawn ones are kept as history.
- Effort: S
