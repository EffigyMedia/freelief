### AUD-043 - The tagline 'Breathe with the circle' shows on every screen, including screens with no circle

- Severity: Low
- Area: F. User experience
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-010. app.js buildShell puts `t("app.tagline")` in the static footer. strings: "app.tagline": "Breathe with the circle. You are safe right now." A 360x640 run printed that tagline on the sort, feedback and ground screens. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: On the colour sort, the feedback page and others, the instruction points to something that is not there. This is a small confusion for a person with little attention.
- Direction: Show the 'Breathe with the circle' part only on the breathe screen, or reword the footer line so that it fits every screen.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-010]].
