### AUD-075 - Visualizer full screen and black screen hide 'Need urgent help?', and the design states no exception to 'every screen'

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (unstated exception to a never-break rule)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. Design F6: "the 'Need urgent help?' control, present on every screen." Section 9 Never break: "the 'Need urgent help?' control on every screen". activities/calm.js: `stage.requestFullscreen?.()` and, for the black screen, `document.body.append(cover)` with a full-screen button. The Decision Log entries for Full screen and Black screen do not mention the help control. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: While full screen or black screen is on, the safety route is one extra step away (leave full screen or tap the black cover). The owner accepted the feature but has not seen this trade-off stated, and no test guards how far away the route is.
- Direction: Either keep a small 'Need urgent help?' control inside the full-screen stage, or record in the Decision Log and F6 that full screen and black screen are the only states without it, and that one tap or key returns to it. Add a test that from either state one action reaches the help control.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-051]].
