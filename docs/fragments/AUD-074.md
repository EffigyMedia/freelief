### AUD-074 - Nothing stops or quiets sound when urgent help opens or when the person leaves the app to call a line

- Severity: Low
- Area: none - omission (actor with no handler)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. app.js helpButton click handler: `showRegion(...); dialog.showModal(); document.documentElement.classList.add("help-is-open"); history.pushState(...)`. It does not touch audio or the current screen. A grep of the app's .js files for `visibilitychange` or `pagehide` finds nothing. audio.js has no suspend or close path. The Visualizer pads and rain loop until `stop()`, and the breathing tones repeat each phase. The help dialog's lines are `tel:` and `sms:` links that hand off to the phone app. Confidence: from the code. How a phone treats Web Audio from a backgrounded tab during a call was not observed this round. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: A person in danger who opens urgent help from the Visualizer or the breathing screen hears music or tones over the crisis-line list. Depending on the device, they may still hear them while they dial or talk to the line. That is a distraction at the most serious moment the app handles.
- Direction: Decide with the owner what happens to sound when urgent help opens and when the page is hidden. For example, fade all sound when the dialog opens, and suspend the AudioContext on `visibilitychange` to hidden. Record the decision in F6. Add a test that opening help from the Visualizer stops the pads.
