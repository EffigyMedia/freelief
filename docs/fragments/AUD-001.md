### AUD-001 - Two other apps on the same origin delete Freelief's offline cache, and Freelief then fails to open offline until its next version

- Severity: Critical
- Area: H. Build & deployment readiness
- Evidence: Quoted this round (UNT-010). The app is served at https://effigymedia.github.io/freelief/ (AGENTS.md). `gh api users/EffigyMedia/repos` lists Pages sites drinax-ref-console, effigy-arcade, freelief, tiny-arcade, so all share the origin effigymedia.github.io and one CacheStorage. The live https://effigymedia.github.io/effigy-arcade/sw.js has `const KEEP = [CORE, RUNTIME];` and, in activate, `await Promise.all(names.map(n => KEEP.includes(n) ? null : caches.delete(n)));`; tiny-arcade/sw.js has the same code, and both also have a 'purge' message that runs `for (const k of await caches.keys()) await caches.delete(k);`. Freelief's sw.js only fills its cache in `install` (`caches.open(CACHE).then((cache) => cache.addAll(FILES))`) and on a miss falls through to `fetch(event.request)`. Scratch proof with the project harness: after install, `caches.keys()` gave ['freelief-0.5.0']; a same-origin delete of every cache not in a KEEP list gave []; then offline reload -> 'Page.reload: net::ERR_FAILED', ready None, guide 0, help control 0. (quoted in round UNT-010)
- Impact: Any person who has opened Effigy Arcade or Tiny Arcade in the same browser loses Freelief's offline use each time either arcade's service worker activates. Freelief then shows a browser error page offline: no breathing guide and no 'Need urgent help?' control. It does not recover until a new Freelief version installs. This breaks REQ-008 (must) and REQ-018/REQ-005 offline, silently, for the people the app exists for.
- Direction: Give Freelief an origin of its own (a custom domain on GitHub Pages, or a separate account or organization Pages site) so no other app shares its CacheStorage. Until then, add a self-heal: on each controlled launch while online, the page or the worker checks `caches.has(CACHE)` and re-runs the precache when it is missing. Add a test that deletes the cache, reloads online, then reloads offline and expects the guide. Tell the Effigy Arcade and Tiny Arcade lines through their inboxes that their activate and purge handlers delete other apps' caches on the shared origin.
- Effort: M
- Found by: the operations lens of round [[UNT-010]].
- **RULED 2026-10-07T14:38:28-04:00** — Attempt 1, UNT-011 (v0.5.1): the owner chose to stay on the shared origin with self-repair. sw.js now handles a 'heal' message that fetches every FILES entry missing from the cache, and app.js posts it on each online launch; the fetch handler also stores network responses. test_offline.py deletes every cache, reloads online, reloads offline, and expects the guide and the help control; the test failed with the heal call switched off and passed with it on. Effigy Arcade and Tiny Arcade were told through their inboxes to limit their activate and purge deletions to their own prefixes. Residual risk, accepted by the owner: if an arcade deletes the cache while the person is offline, Freelief cannot repair until it is online again. A later round confirms.
