### AUD-072 - Each commit carries a store index that lags its own fragments: at HEAD it shows UNT-050 open and RLG-025 requested, and it omits UNT-051

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. At b1a7858 the committed docs/fragments/.index.json (generated "2026-10-08T01:03:09-04:00") lists UNT-050 with 'status': 'open' and RLG-025 with 'status': 'requested', and has no entry for UNT-051. The files say UNT-050 status = "closed" (closed 01:03:16) and RLG-025 status = "built". At 283cbf8 the index (generated 00:24:26) lists RLG-018 and RLG-024 as 'requested', although both were built, and it lacks UNT-047 to UNT-049. Audit_and_Testing.md section 5 tells every round to "Read the index, not every fragment." (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The index that the audit method and later sessions read first is wrong about the latest unit and tracker item, and the lag repeats commit after commit. A session or a lens that reads the index, as the method directs, sees built work as open and misses recent units. This round had to read the fragments to find the true state.
- Direction: Regenerate the index after the last fragment change of a unit and before its commit: make unit close regenerate it, or make a commit-time check fail when the index stamp does not match the fragments. Regenerate it now. If the cause is in the environment's fragment tool, send it to the environment inbox.
