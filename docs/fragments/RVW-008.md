**Closing:** This is not sound as written, for three reasons. First, of the 18 earlier findings in this brief, only AUD-018, AUD-043 and AUD-044 are done; 5 are partly done and 10 are not done. Second, this round raises 11 new findings. Seven are Medium: Freelief opens on the menu, but AGENTS.md, the design and the README still say that it opens on breathing; the research record has drifted from research.json; the Visualizer breaks the activity storage boundary; the privacy text leaves out stored settings; work after slice 5 belongs to no slice; and the 1.0 criteria still require 150 KB. Third, on the question of whether the long out-breath is better than box breathing: Balban 2023 did not test that, and 4-in 6-out was not one of its arms. The Standards text and the default-rhythm decision claim more than the trial shows.
