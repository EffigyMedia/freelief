### REQ-029 — A standard is shown as met only when an automated check of every page passes and a manual check with a screen reader and with a keyboard alone is recorded, with its date and tester, for the version shown or for an earlier version whose interface (its screens, styles, text and shell) is unchanged since. The manual check may come from community volunteers.

**History.**
- 2026-10-09 (owner, AUD-102; UNT-099): was "...recorded, with its date and tester, for the version shown." The record of a check ships in the version after the checked one, so under the old text no check could ever show. The owner chose: a check holds until a release changes the interface. `tools/freelief.py claim_outdated()` decides it, and the tests and `build --release` refuse an outdated entry.
