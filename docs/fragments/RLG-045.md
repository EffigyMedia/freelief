### RLG-045 — Music and nature sound persist across screens until turned off, with an Off choice; rethink the Visualizer's name and design
- **RULED 2026-10-09T06:23:52-04:00** — Owner, 2026-10-09: the music and nature sounds should keep playing until turned off, so an Off choice is needed; the owner asks whether this means renaming the Visualizer or designing it differently. Next session: propose 2 or 3 designs with the question tool before building, for example (a) a global 'Background sound: Off / Music / Nature / Both' control (header or Settings) that plays on every screen, with the Visualizer kept as shapes only and perhaps renamed; (b) the Visualizer renamed 'Sounds' and its choice persisting after you leave it, with an Off choice; (c) a small player bar under the header while a sound plays. It interacts with the header speaker (mute stays a master fade), breathing tones (mix or duck), urgent help (fade out), and RLG-043 (Nature with rain or waves). Build it together with RLG-043.
