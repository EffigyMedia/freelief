### AUD-069 - The Visualizer, the vibration module and the start-up fallback have no place in the design's vocabulary, flows or module table, or in AGENTS.md Architecture

- Severity: Low
- Area: G. Missing documentation
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. Stage 8 module table, activities row: "`activities/bubbles.js`, `trace.js`, `sort.js`, `ripple.js`, `mandala.js`". It omits activities/calm.js, and it has no rows for haptics.js or fallback.js, which both ship. Stage 4 Activity: "the bubble field, the shape trace, the colour sort, the ripple pond, or mandala coloring". The Visualizer is not named, though the product overview says "and the Visualizer (music or rain with soft shapes)". F5 describes five activities and has no Visualizer flow. F7 does not list Vibration or the region. AGENTS.md Architecture names neither haptics.js, audio.js, motion.js nor fallback.js. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: Three shipped modules have no stated owner or 'must not'. A reader cannot tell whether the Visualizer is an Activity, which affects the rules it must keep (no timer: calm.js runs setTimeout loops for its shapes, and that may be fine, but nothing says so).
- Direction: Add calm.js, haptics.js and fallback.js to the Stage 8 table, each with what it owns and what it must not do. Define the Visualizer in Stage 4 (as an Activity, or as its own term), add it to F5, and add Vibration and the region to F7. Mirror the module list in AGENTS.md Architecture together with the AUD-042 fix.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the whole-design lens of round [[UNT-051]].
