### AUD-063 - docs/research/sources.md, the written research record, is out of step with data/research.json: it lacks eight cited sources and their evidence notes, keeps removed techniques, and now makes a false statement about the colour sort

- Severity: Medium
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. data/research.json lists 17 sources (16 cited), all "checked" 2026-10-08. A comparison with sources.md finds 8 absent: pallavicini2021, engelhard2010, lee2013, holmes2009, james2015, white2010, vandervennet2012 and koo2020. sources.md dates are all "checked 2026-10-07", and git shows that its last change was 79e10c1 (v0.5.9). It still has full sections for "## 5-4-3-2-1 grounding" and "## Calming statements", which were removed (UNT-032), with no withdrawn note, and it has no ripple or mandala section. Its last paragraph says: "The colour sort ... rests on the same sources as the bubble field and the shape trace (7, 8 and 9), and the Standards & research page lists it there." The page now cites holmes2009 and james2015 for sort. trust_pages.md: "The full notes, with the strength of each source, are in `docs/research/sources.md`; keep the three in step." Design Stage 7 row 6 names `docs/research/` as the home of the research sources. The Decision Log (UNT-049) says that every source was checked in the session. Its Balban note records no comparison with box, yet the app now says (standards.evidence.breathing) "more than box breathing"; that reading from the UNT-049 source check exists only in the RLG-018 ruling and the 283cbf8 commit message. Confidence (omission lens): it did not check whether the cyclic-sighing and box difference was statistically significant. (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The project's evidence record and the page it backs say different things. The rule 'claims are only what is verified' (REQ-025, REQ-024) depends on a record that a later session can check. A reviewer or a later session that audits a claim against sources.md finds no record of eight sources, wrong dates, the wrong technique set, and a sentence that is now false. The default rhythm decision and a comparative evidence claim shown to users rest on a check that left no notes. The strength notes for the new sources exist only in user-facing strings.
- Direction: Bring sources.md into step with research.json. Give each of the 16 cited sources a dated evidence note (2026-10-08 for the new eight) that states what was tested and on whom. Move grounding and calming statements under a 'removed' heading (UNT-032) or delete them. Replace the colour-sort paragraph. Write the Balban note to say exactly what the trial compared, including whether the cyclic-sighing and box difference was significant, and make standards.evidence.breathing say no more than that note. Add a test that every source id in research.json appears in sources.md, so the two cannot drift again.
