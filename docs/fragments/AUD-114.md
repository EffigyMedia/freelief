### AUD-114 - Reset settings starts the background music and turns sound back on, from the Settings screen, for a person who had stopped the music or silenced Freelief, with no warning

- Severity: Medium
- Area: F. User experience (no surprise sound)
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-104. app.js onSettingChange: `if (name === "calmMode") background.play(value);`. settings.js resetSettings() calls every listener for each value that changes back (`changed.forEach((name) => listeners.forEach((listener) => listener(name, values[name])))`). config.json `calm.defaultMode` is "music", and `sounds.enabledByDefault` is true. Whole-design probe (harness.open_app with the OSC_PROBE from test_calm.py): on the Visualizer, choose Off, then Settings, Reset settings, "Yes, reset". Output: "after Off: live pads 0 stored {"calmMode":"off"}" and "after Reset: live pads 10 bar visible True bar text Playing: music". Omission probe (omission_lens_reset.py): store {"calmMode":"off","sounds":false}, open #settings, press Reset settings, then Yes, reset. Result: 'speaker pressed before: false', 'question: Reset every setting, including your country for urgent help?', 'stored after: {}', 'screen: settings', 'speaker pressed after: true', 'live pads: 10', 'sound bar: False Playing: music', no console errors. The design says: "The background does not start when the app opens; it starts when the person opens the Visualizer" (Decision Log, RLG-045). Section 6: "No timer, no score, no failure, no surprise sound, no sudden motion." REQ-036: the sound "keeps playing on every screen until the person chooses Off or presses Stop". test_reset_settings_brings_back_every_default_and_a_default_choice_is_not_stored checks only the stored values (rhythm and theme). (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: A person who muted Freelief in public, or who pressed Stop or chose Off on the music, and then resets Settings gets music at once on a screen that never plays sound. Reset also turns sound back on when it was off. The confirmation question names only the country for urgent help, so nothing warns them. For an anxious person, or a person with sound sensitivity in a crisis, this is the surprise sound that the design rules out and that the speaker button and the fade were added to prevent. The sound bar has no live region (RLG-047), so a screen-reader user hears music start with no announcement. The reach is limited to people who use Reset.
- Direction: Make Reset change the stored choice and not playback. Reset calmMode to the default but do not start the background sound: keep the background as it is (or stop it), and start it only when the person opens the Visualizer or makes an explicit choice, as the RLG-045 decision says. Decide whether Reset may turn sound back on: either leave the mute state alone, or name it in settings.resetQuestion ("sound comes back on"). Add a test: store calmMode off and sounds false, reset, and assert that no oscillator or pad is live and that the sound bar stays hidden.
- Effort: S
