### AUD-125 - Design section 6 promises 44 px targets with no exception while the mandala decision accepts 24 px, and RLG-047 bundles that open owner decision with about fifteen other unfixed interface findings, so no one of them can be ruled on alone

- Severity: Low
- Area: A. Design alignment; G. Process amendments
- Evidence: Design section 6: "Large targets (at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, larger for the main actions)." Decision Log (mandala): "Every shape in every design is at least 24 px at a 360 px width (WCAG 2.5.8)", and again for the eight designs: "stays at least 24 px at a 360 px width". RLG-047 (status requested, an orphan in checks.py): "(1) MEDIUM, owner decision: mandala parts in the denser designs are about 37-40 px on a phone, under the stress checks' 44 px ... Options: thin the densest rings, or accept. (2) MEDIUM, owner check: the pond redraws about 1,200 to 2,700 SVG dots ... (3) LOW ... (4) LOW: theme-color does not follow a forced theme; document.title is the same on every screen; ... the sound bar has no live region; ... Start again, Next board and New mandala have no undo; ...". REQ-016 asks for AAA "where a criterion can be met". Design principle 2 says "shaky hands are normal use". Audit_and_Testing.md section 10: "Anything an approval can name must be atomic. If a person can sign off on a named thing ... that thing must mean exactly one thing." Design slice 8 Done when: "those are built or ruled on". (quoted in round UNT-104)
- Impact: The design disagrees with itself about the target size. A reader of section 6 believes every target is 44 px, and a person with shaky hands meets 37 to 40 px mandala parts. The owner's choice on the mandala target size, a Medium accessibility question, has no record of its own and is not in the thread's next action. The owner cannot approve or decline it without also ruling on a dozen unrelated items. A ruling on RLG-047 cannot say which of its items it accepts, so slice 8's "built or ruled on" cannot be judged, and the live sound-bar gap stays hidden among cosmetic items.
- Direction: Split RLG-047: one record for the mandala size decision, put to the owner with the question tool, and one for each item that will be fixed or declined. Keep RLG-047 as the review report that cites them. Add item (2) to RLG-033, which it already names. After the owner rules, either write the exception into section 6 (activity shapes may be 24 px or more, with the reason) or thin the densest rings to 44 px, and delete whichever statement loses.
