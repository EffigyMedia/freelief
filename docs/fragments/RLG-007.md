### RLG-007 — Drag in the colour sort, as an addition to choose-then-swap
- **RULED 2026-10-07T22:40:04-04:00** — Built in UNT-029 as v0.5.16. Drag is an addition to choose-then-swap, as the owner asked: a drop on another tile swaps the two and the live line says the same words as a tap swap. A movement under sort.dragThreshold (8 pixels, config.json) is a tap; a drop off the tiles changes nothing; the tiles carry touch-action none so a drag does not scroll the page. Tests: a mouse drag swaps, a solve by drag alone, a drop off the tiles, a small slip still chooses and a drag does not swallow the next click, and a touch drag by CDP touch events. The keyboard solve and axe in both themes still pass. 87 tests; bench 139.8 ms, 59.1 ms, 179.3 KB. How a drag feels on a phone is not something a test can observe: the owner checks it on a phone.
