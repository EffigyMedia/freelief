### AUD-065 - The design keeps superseded figures and rules after the owner's changes, so the 1.0 release criteria require a 150 KB size that REQ-028 replaced, and F2, the risk table, Settings, deploy and requirement-range clauses are stale

- Severity: Medium
- Area: G. Documentation quality
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-051. Stage 2 success criteria: "The whole app is under 150 KB (REQ-028)." Stage 9: "REQ-028 (150 KB)". srs-assumptions: "a plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript app under 150 KB". These are regenerated into PRD.md ("The whole app is under 150 KB (REQ-028).") and SRS.md. REQ-028 is now 250 KB (Decision Log), and build reports "175.0 KB of 250 KB". Stage 10: "Release criteria for 1.0: ... every success criterion in Stage 2 holds". The Stage 11 risk table: "tones are off by default (REQ-007)", but sounds are on by default. F2: "'More ways to calm', which every screen except the menu shows, beside 'Back to breathing'. ... Leaving any exercise returns to the breath guide." The shell has only `nav.backMenu` ("Back to menu"), and strings/en.json has no 'More ways to calm' or 'Back to breathing' key. Stages 5 and 13: "The requirements are the records `REQ-001` to `REQ-031`", but REQ-032 to REQ-034 exist. Section 7 row 7 says "GitHub Pages serves `main` ... A push deploys." Section 4 Settings lists only "rhythm, tones, and theme". REQ-007's text still names "a chime on Next" from the removed grounding screen. (The breathing-first F1, Stage 2 zero-taps and Stage 8 routing clauses, which the omission lens also listed here, are the menu-first finding.) (quoted in round UNT-051)
- Impact: The design is the contract each audit and each fix is judged against. As written, the release gate for 1.0 cannot pass: one criterion fails at 175 KB. Readers of the design meet two answers on size, sound defaults, navigation and deploy branch, with nothing to say which is live, and a reader of F2 builds or tests the wrong flow. This is the exact pattern that Audit_and_Testing section 10 forbids ("Every fix names the sentence it deletes and deletes it in the same edit"), and AGENTS.md says "code and docs must never disagree."
- Direction: Make one pass over the design document, restating each clause against the Decision Log entry that changed it and keeping the dated change notes. Replace 150 KB with 250 KB in Stage 2, Stage 9 and the assumptions. Fix the Stage 11 risk row, F2, section 7 row 7, the section 4 Settings definition, and the requirement range in Stages 5 and 13 (to REQ-034). Remove "a chime on Next" from REQ-007 with a history line. Then regenerate the SRS and the PRD. Add a repo test that the design's size figure equals the REQ-028 figure.
