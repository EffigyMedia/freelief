### AUD-109 - The Visualizer and the wake lock were built with no requirement record

- Severity: Low
- Area: A. Requirement compliance
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082. Design section 5: "The requirements are the records `REQ-001` to `REQ-034`. Their priority is the feature list." Every other activity has its own record: REQ-012 bubbles, REQ-013 trace, REQ-014 sort, REQ-032 ripple, REQ-033 mandala. A search of REQ-*.md for "visualizer" or "wake" finds nothing. REQ-004 covers "interactive calming activities", but the Visualizer is "The activity with nothing to do". (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: The Visualizer and the safety wake lock have no priority, no verification method and no line in the generated SRS. Nothing says whether 1.0 needs them, and a change to them has no record to amend.
- Direction: Add a REQ- record for the Visualizer and one for the screen wake lock, each with priority and verification, and regenerate the SRS and PRD.
