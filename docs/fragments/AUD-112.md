### AUD-112 - Nothing tells a person when the offline copy is complete, so someone who installs Freelief for a future crisis cannot know it works offline

- Severity: Low
- Area: F. User experience
- Evidence: Quoted in round UNT-082. F1: "*first visit online* — the service worker installs and caches the app in the background, and the menu does not wait for it". sw.js precache has no message to the page on success. app.js listens only for `event.data.heal === false`. strings/en.json has no text for offline-ready. Confidence note: whether people need this is a judgment for the owner. (quoted in round UNT-082)
- Impact: A person who opens Freelief once and then goes offline before the precache finishes, for example on a slow network, has an app that does not open offline when they need it. They had no way to know.
- Direction: Design concern for the owner. Decide whether to show a quiet "Ready to use offline" line, for example in Settings next to the version, once the version's cache is complete. If the owner decides yes, add the text to strings and add a test.
- Effort: S
- Found by: the omission lens of round [[UNT-082]].
