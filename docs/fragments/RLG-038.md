### RLG-038 — A sound stopped while muted must not return when sound comes back
- **RULED 2026-10-08T22:12:17-04:00** — Built in UNT-085 as v0.7.2. audio.js registers each fading sound (pads, rain, breath tone) with a cut-off; applySound(false) runs every cut-off just before it suspends the context, and a sound stopped while the context is already paused disconnects and stops at once (pads, rain, glass, breath tone). Test: mute in the Visualizer, leave it; the fading music's gain was disconnected and no stop is left scheduled; it fails without the cut-off. One full run had a single failure of test_each_activity_event_gives_one_short_pulse, which passed three times alone and in the next full run: a timing flake on a loaded machine, to watch. 152 tests.
